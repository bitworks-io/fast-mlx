Methodology

Compare the same thing, or do not declare a winner.

Every result is scoped to exact source, dependency, model, tokenizer, workload, route, hardware, and measurement identities. Mismatched or incomplete cells remain context—not a public performance conclusion.

Correctness before speed

Exact paths must reproduce the required token and state behavior. Lossy paths are compared against a source-locked reference with teacher-forced distribution metrics and task checks. A fluent sample is not a quality measurement.

Whole-request measurement

Decode throughput alone can hide prefill regressions, request-start latency, memory pressure, admission failures, or service-level contention. The applicable gate includes the user-visible request boundary and operating mode.

Public claim boundary

The public site reports fast-mlx's own dated results. Private ecosystem research may inform priorities, but it is not projected into competitor scorecards. Raw evidence with local paths, host identity, incomplete runs, or unreviewed claims stays outside the public repository.
